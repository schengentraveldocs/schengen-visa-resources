France – 7-Day Tourist Travel Itinerary

Destination:Paris, France  
Duration:7 Days / 6 Nights  
Purpose:Tourism and Sightseeing  
Accommodation:Paris, France

Day 1 – Arrival in Paris

- Arrive in Paris.
- Transfer to the hotel and complete check-in.
- Rest after the journey.
- Enjoy a short walk around the hotel neighbourhood.
- Overnight stay in Paris.

Day 2 – Eiffel Tower and Seine River

- Visit the Eiffel Tower.
- Explore Champ de Mars.
- Enjoy the Trocadéro gardens and viewpoints.
- Take a sightseeing walk along the River Seine.
- Overnight stay in Paris.

Day 3 – Louvre Museum and Historic Paris

- Visit the Louvre Museum.
- Explore the Tuileries Garden.
- Visit Place de la Concorde.
- Walk along the Champs-Élysées.
- Overnight stay in Paris.

Day 4 – Notre-Dame and Montmartre

- Visit Notre-Dame Cathedral.
- Explore Île de la Cité.
- Visit Montmartre.
- Explore Sacré-Cœur Basilica.
- Enjoy the artistic surroundings of Place du Tertre.
- Overnight stay in Paris.

Day 5 – Versailles Day Trip

- Travel to the Palace of Versailles.
- Explore the palace and its historic interiors.
- Visit the gardens of Versailles.
- Return to Paris.
- Overnight stay in Paris.

Day 6 – Culture and Leisure

- Visit Musée d'Orsay.
- Explore Saint-Germain-des-Prés.
- Enjoy local cafés and shopping.
- Spend the afternoon exploring Paris at leisure.
- Overnight stay in Paris.

Day 7 – Departure

- Have breakfast at the hotel.
- Complete hotel check-out.
- Transfer to the airport.
- Depart France according to the confirmed travel arrangements.
