Employment and Financial Circumstances

I am currently employed as [Job Title] at [Company Name]. My employment and financial circumstances are supported by the relevant employment confirmation, income evidence and bank statements submitted with my application.

Travel Arrangements

My proposed travel arrangements, including flight reservations and travel medical insurance, are included in my application documents.

Intention to Return

I intend to return to [Country of Residence] following my planned visit. My employment, personal circumstances and other relevant supporting documents demonstrate my reasons for returning.

Closing Statement

I respectfully request that you consider my application for a short-stay Schengen tourist visa.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
