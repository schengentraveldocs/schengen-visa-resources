Schengen Visa Documentation Checklist

Tourist Visa – Short-Stay (Type C)

This checklist provides a general overview of documents commonly required when preparing a Schengen short-stay tourist visa application.

1. Personal Documents

Completed Schengen visa application form

Valid passport

Copies of previous visas, where applicable

Recent passport-size photographs meeting the required specifications

Proof of legal residence, where applicable

2. Financial Documents

Recent bank statements

Proof of income

Recent salary slips, where applicable

Sponsorship documents, if applicable

3. Employment Documents

Employment confirmation letter

Approved leave letter

Employment contract, where applicable

Business registration and supporting documents for self-employed applicants

4. Travel Documents

Flight reservation or travel itinerary

Hotel reservation or proof of accommodation

Day-by-day travel itinerary

Travel medical insurance meeting Schengen requirements

5. Additional Supporting Documents

Cover letter explaining the purpose of travel

Evidence of sufficient financial means

Documents demonstrating the applicant's circumstances and intention to return

Invitation letter, if applicable

Important Notes

Requirements vary depending on the applicant's nationality, country of residence, destination country and individual circumstances.

Applicants should always consult the relevant embassy or consulate's official checklist before submitting an application.
